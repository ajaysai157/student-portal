Accounts

Role based access

Administrators, faculty and students each see their own work.

One portal, three roles

Access is decided by the account that signs in - never by something a visitor can type into a form.

Administrator

Full control over the college records.

  • Register, edit and delete student records
  • Create departments and assign faculty to them
  • Create faculty and student login accounts
  • Review correction requests from students

Faculty member

College wide visibility, read only student data.

  • Browse every student with search and filters
  • Read student profiles and photographs
  • Update the description, office and email of their own departments

Student

A small self service area for their own record.

  • See their own ID, department, section and photo
  • Read the public details of their department
  • Request a correction without changing the record directly
